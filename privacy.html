Fincopilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Fincopilot is a personal financial management and investment tracking application developed for personal, non-commercial use.

Financial Data

Fincopilot may access financial account information through Enable Banking when explicitly authorized by the account owner.

Financial data is used for personal financial tracking, analysis, budgeting, and investment management.

Data Sharing

Financial information is not sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

Account Access

Access to connected financial accounts can be revoked by the account owner through the relevant financial institution or Enable Banking.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the application owner.
